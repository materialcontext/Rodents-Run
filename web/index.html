      How to Play

      You are the mouse. Push the blocks to trap the cats. Walk into a block to push it, and the whole row behind it moves, as long as there is room at the far end. Walls never move.

      When every cat on the board is stuck at the same time, they all turn into cheese. Eat it for 100 points (25 more for each level after the first).

      The stopwatch runs for the whole level. When the minute hand reaches the blue mark more cats arrive. Trapping all the cats races the clock on to the next batch; trap them after the last batch and the level is won, with a bonus.

      Later levels add sink holes (they hold you for a moment, and swallow pushed blocks), mouse traps (deadly) and balls of yarn that lurk in the walls and roll at you.

      You have three mice. Faster speeds and quick trapping score more.

      Touch

      Use the pad: touch anywhere in the area around it and slide your thumb to aim, including diagonals. Hold to keep walking. Or swipe across the field: each short drag is one step.
      About Rodent's Revenge

      Rodent's Revenge
      A remake for phones and browsers.

      The original was written by Christopher Lee Fraley and released by Microsoft in Entertainment Pack 2 (1991). Its rules here follow the original program's logic as recovered from its code; the graphics are new drawings in the same style.

      Game rules in Rust, compiled to WebAssembly.
